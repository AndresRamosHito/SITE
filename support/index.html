Support · Orchidarc / OrquIDea

Support

Use this page for OrquIDea app support, account questions, conservation safety reports, and data requests. It is intended to serve as the Support URL for future App Store and Google Play listings.

Do not publish exact locations of sensitive orchids. If you need to report illegal extraction or a vulnerable population, contact Orchidarc privately.

App support

For login problems, upload issues, observation corrections, or technical problems, email Orchidarc with a short description and screenshots if useful.

Email support

Conservation reports

Report suspected illegal collection, orchid trade, disturbance, or unsafe location disclosure through the app where available or by email.

  • Do not post exact locations publicly.
  • Include general area, date, and evidence if safe.
  • Do not confront people in the field.

Data requests

For account deletion, privacy requests, record correction, or removal requests, contact Orchidarc from the email associated with your account.

Read privacy policy

Field-use reminder

OrquIDea is for observation, photography, documentation, education, and conservation. It must not be used to locate, collect, remove, trade, or disturb wild orchids.
