Striking Gold:
Mexican Cypripedium

The largest known population of Mexican Cypripedium and what it teaches us.

Andrés Ramos · Orchidarc

Primary taxon
Cypripedium irapeanum

In the summer of 2023, a casual browse through the online forum of the Asociación Mexicana de Orquideología turned into one of the most significant discoveries in Orchidarc’s short history.

The post, shared by the naturalist Gabriel, led Orchidarc to a single mountain in Veracruz. Spread across the slopes was a vast population of golden-flowered Cypripedium, estimated at nearly 5,000 individuals.

One mountain, three habitats

The orchids occupied oak savannah, open grassland and rocky summit habitat, producing dramatically different growth forms linked to microclimate and exposure.

Fire and recovery

Wildfires swept across the region in 2024, damaging forest structure but also revealing the resilience of the underground rhizomes, which regenerated strongly during the following rainy season.

Conservation

Seed pods collected during fieldwork were entrusted to Dr. David Gregory at the University of Sheffield, where successful germination and early growth have now been achieved under laboratory conditions.
