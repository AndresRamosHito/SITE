Species profile · Jalisco field record

Laelia eyermaniana

A western Mexican Laelia, shown here in the white-flowered alba form documented during Orchidarc field work and featured in Encinos y Legados.

Accepted nameLaelia eyermaniana
AuthorRchb.f.
HabitPseudobulbous epiphyte
Native rangeNorthern & western Mexico

This plate documents the white-flowered alba form of Laelia eyermaniana. The species is accepted by Plants of the World Online and is native to northern and western Mexico.

The Orchidarc field material illustrated here comes from the Jalisco work associated with Encinos y Legados. The plate combines floral morphology, pollinarium details, habit, dissected perianth segments and a documented Bombus visitor carrying orchid pollinia.

Taxonomy and range

Laelia eyermaniana was published by H. G. Reichenbach in 1888. Current Kew treatment accepts the name and places its native range in northern and western Mexico. The alba material on this page is presented as a colour form within the species rather than as a separately assessed taxon.
