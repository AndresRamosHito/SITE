Lepanthes salvaterrae belongs to one of the most diverse and delicate orchid lineages in the Neotropics. Like many members of the genus, it is a miniature epiphyte, easily overlooked in the field but ecologically tied to humid bark surfaces, moss layers, and the stable microclimate of cloud forest.

Field recognition

The species is treated here according to the 2025 description by Solano et al. Orchidarc records emphasize its field appearance, its small stature, and its occurrence in humid cloud-forest conditions. The full formal diagnosis should be checked against the original taxonomic publication when preparing scientific or legal conservation material.

Taxonomic note. Earlier Orchidarc herbarium entries listed several images as unidentified Lepanthes forms. These are now treated here as Lepanthes salvaterrae and grouped on this species profile.

Habitat and conservation

Miniature Lepanthes species can be highly sensitive to changes in humidity, canopy cover, and host-tree continuity. Their small size does not make them ecologically minor: they are useful indicators of intact cloud-forest microhabitats and of the persistence of bryophyte-rich epiphytic communities.
